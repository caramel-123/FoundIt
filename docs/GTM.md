# Go to market

Channels and dates are assumptions for a first-semester pilot at PUP Main.

## Launch audience
The first 100 users are students in the Facebook groups and group chats where lost
item posts already happen, plus the admin office staff who hold found items.

## Channels
1. Reply to lost and found posts in PUP Facebook groups with the FoundIt link (free, where the demand already is).
2. QR posters at the admin office, library, gym, canteen and main entrance.
3. Student council and org pages announcing the pilot.

## Messaging
- One line: "Lost it on campus? Foundit probably has it."
- Proof: every item is verified through the admin office, and only the real owner can answer the form.
- Call to action: "Post it. Verify it. Return it." with the link and QR.

## Launch plan
| Week | Plan |
|---|---|
| 1 | Onboard admin office staff; they log items already in the office box |
| 2 | Posters and QR codes up; announce through the student council |
| 3 | Reply to every lost-item post in the groups with a FoundIt link |
| 4 | Share the first returns (with permission) as proof; collect feedback |

## Metrics
Weekly active users, items posted, items returned, and time to first comment on a
lost post. Targets are in docs/BRD.md.
