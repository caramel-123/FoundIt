# Problem statement

## Idea in one sentence
FoundIt puts PUP's lost-and-found online so finders can post items in seconds and
owners can prove an item is theirs before it is handed back.

## Who has the problem
- Students who lose IDs, wallets, phones, keys and tumblers on campus.
- Students who find things and want to return them without the hassle.
- The admin office, which holds turned-in items that nobody knows are there.

## How often and what it costs
Losses happen daily on a campus the size of PUP Main. A lost school ID blocks
entry and needs a replacement fee and a wait. A lost phone or wallet can mean lost
money, cards and data. The cost to finders is time, since returning an item well
takes effort.

## What they do today
- Post in Facebook groups, freedom walls and class group chats, where posts get
  buried within hours.
- Walk to the admin office and ask, with no way to check from a phone first.
- Trust whoever claims the item, with no proof of ownership.

## Top three assumptions and a test for each
1. Students would check a single feed before asking around. Test by counting how
   many items get a comment or claim within 48 hours in a pilot.
2. Finders will spend under a minute logging an item. Test by timing ten first-time
   posts from the composer (R1, R12).
3. Owners accept answering verification questions to prove ownership. Test by
   tracking how many "I found it" forms get an owner answer (R16, R17).
