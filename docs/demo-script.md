# Demo script

Three minutes live, or the 30 second video (design/video/foundit-motion.mp4).
Each step cites its requirement id.

| Time | Screen | Say | Do | R |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Landing | "Lost items at PUP get buried in group chats or sit in an office box nobody sees." | Open the app | R10a |
| 0:20 | Sign in | "Sign in with your school Google account." | Continue with Google | R10 |
| 0:30 | Feed | "One campus feed for every lost and found item." | Scroll the feed | R3, R4 |
| 0:50 | Composer | "Found something? Write one caption, pick the place, add a photo." | Post a found item | R1 |
| 1:10 | Caption import | "Or paste a caption from a group chat and it fills itself in." | Open the import pop-up | R12 |
| 1:25 | Lost post | "Someone lost their ID. I found it, so I send a form only the owner can answer." | Tap "I found it", create the form | R17 |
| 1:50 | Alerts | "The owner gets notified and answers." | Open the notification | R18 |
| 2:10 | Verification | "I check the answers and approve. Pickup happens at the admin office." | Approve | R16, R2 |
| 2:30 | Profile | "Every author has a profile, so you know who you are dealing with." | Tap an avatar | R5c |
| 2:45 | Close | "Post it. Verify it. Return it." | Back to the feed | |

## Fallback
If the network or app fails, play design/video/foundit-motion.mp4 and walk through
