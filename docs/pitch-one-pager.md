# FoundIt pitch one-pager

## Problem
Lost items at PUP end up in group chats that bury them or in an office box nobody
sees, and anyone can claim to be the owner.

## Solution
One campus feed for lost and found items. Post with a photo and a real campus place,
answer or send a verification form, and pick up at the admin office.

Key actions
1. Post a found or lost item in seconds, even offline (R1, R12).
2. Verify the owner with questions only they can answer (R16, R17).
3. Return through the admin office, with staff tracking custody (R2, R8).

## Why now
Every student already has a phone and uses campus Facebook groups for this, which
shows the demand. Cheap hosted databases and AI APIs make a verified, shared feed
buildable by a small team.

## Who it is for
PUP Main students first, then faculty and staff, then other campuses.

## Traction or proof
None yet. The app is built and working (Supabase backend, installable on phones)
and ready for a pilot.

## Ask
Endorsement from the admin office and student council for a one-semester pilot,
and a place on the official campus pages.
